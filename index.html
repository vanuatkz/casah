Who We Are

A University-Wide Harvard Community

CASA Harvard is a university-wide student organization representing and uniting Harvard students from Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, and other Turkic nations of related heritage.

We respond to the social and cultural needs of Central Asian students — connecting undergraduates, graduate students, MPA, MBA, and Ph.D. scholars with one another and with the broader Harvard community of faculty, alumni, and local communities.

Through education, culture, and dialogue, we promote cross-cultural awareness rooted in mutual respect and deep appreciation of our region's rich heritage.

Our Mission

"Sharing and appreciating our common values, promoting cross-cultural awareness rooted in mutual respect and understanding of Central Asian cultures within Harvard."

CASA Harvard organizes educational and cultural activities, seminars, professional and personal development courses, guest lectures, festivals, art performances, and cultural film screenings. Through events that bring Central Asia to the fore and stimulate further dialogue, we reach students across Harvard University and bring the culture of Central Asia to life.

What We Do

Our Pillars

🎓

Academic & Professional

Seminars, guest lectures, career workshops, and mentorship opportunities connecting students with Central Asian scholars, alumni, and professionals.

🌸

Cultural Celebration

Annual Nowruz festivals, traditional art performances, food events, and cultural showcases that bring the richness of Central Asia to the Harvard campus.

🤝

Community & Belonging

A welcoming home for all Harvard students — undergraduate, graduate, and professional — connected by their Central Asian heritage or shared curiosity.

🎬

Arts & Media

Cultural film screenings, photography exhibitions, and artistic presentations that illuminate the history and contemporary life of Central Asia.

Highlights

Signature Events

🌸

Nowruz at Harvard

Our annual Persian New Year celebration — Nowruz — is CASA Harvard's flagship event, filling Sanders Theatre with music, dance, food, and the spirit of spring renewal shared across Central Asia.

🎙️

Guest Lectures & Panels

We regularly host scholars, diplomats, entrepreneurs, and alumni from across the Turkic world for conversations on politics, business, culture, and the future of Central Asia.

🍽️

Cultural Dinners

From plov to beshbarmak, our cultural food events offer a delicious gateway to the traditions and hospitality that define Central Asian culture.
